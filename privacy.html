Privacy Policy

How On The Verge Roofing Ltd collects and uses information submitted through this website.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who We Are

This website is operated by On The Verge Roofing Ltd. We provide roofing services across Wrexham, Chester, North Wales, Shropshire and surrounding areas.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at ben@onthevergeroofing.com .

2. Information We Collect

When you use the contact form on this website, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your telephone number
  • Your email address, if provided
  • Information about the roofing work you require
  • Any other information you choose to include in your message

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the contact form is used to respond to your enquiry, discuss your roofing requirements and provide quotations or information about our services.

We do not collect information through the contact form for unrelated marketing purposes.

4. Contact Form Provider

Our website contact form is processed using Formspree. Information submitted through the form is transmitted to Formspree so that we can receive and respond to your enquiry.

You can find more information about how Formspree handles submitted information in its own privacy documentation.

5. How Long We Keep Information

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with your enquiry, provide our services and maintain appropriate business records.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may use service providers, such as our website contact form provider, where necessary to operate the website and receive enquiries.

7. Your Rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights regarding your personal information. These may include requesting access to information we hold about you, asking for inaccurate information to be corrected, or asking us to delete information where appropriate.

To make a request or ask a question about your information, contact us at ben@onthevergeroofing.com .

8. Cookies

This website does not intentionally use cookies for advertising or personalised marketing.

Some third-party services used by the website may operate their own technical cookies or similar technologies where required to provide their service.

9. Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, our services or applicable legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page.
